## Descriptive text relevant to family ordering

Printed page 124 (PDF 134) states that smaller separation moves humps and
hollows to higher Froude number. It then states that a smaller `L/B` also gives
a higher Froude number for those features, and that at `S/L:0.2` and `0.3` a
higher `L/B` gives smaller wave-interference amplitude; at `S/L:0.5`, the
interference for `L/B:9` and `L/B:11` is described as practically the same.
Printed page 125 (PDF 135) says the monohull-wave-pattern prediction is mainly
dependent on separation rather than hull shape, with a small phase shift as
`L/B` changes.
