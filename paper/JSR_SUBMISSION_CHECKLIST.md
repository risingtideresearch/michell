## Manual decisions and confirmations before submission

1. Choose the corresponding author and supply that person's email, postal
   address, telephone number if requested, and SNAME membership grade if any.
2. Obtain both authors' approval of the exact submitted PDF and confirm that
   the work is original, is not under review elsewhere, and has any required
   foundation approval.
3. Obtain and review de Sendagorta and Grases (1988), DOI
   `10.5957/jsr.1988.32.1.19`, or retain the manuscript's explicit uncertainty.
4. Mint the Zenodo DOI by following `ARCHIVE_CHECKLIST.md`; replace
   `10.5281/zenodo.REPLACE-ME` and rebuild before upload.
5. Confirm conflicts of interest, funding, author contributions, permissions,
   and any suggested/opposed reviewers requested by the live ScholarOne form.
6. Confirm the public JSR blinding and generative-AI requirements in the live
   portal. Record any rule not present in the public SNAME documents.
7. Proofread the converted submission PDF generated by ScholarOne, checking
   every equation, table, figure, hyperlink, and page.
8. Submit only after replacing every bracketed field in `COVER_LETTER_JSR.md`.
