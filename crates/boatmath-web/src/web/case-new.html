Inertia and damping

These matter only in waves. Defaults: kyy 0.25 L; kxx each hull's mass at its centreplane plus 0.35 of its beam; kzz the hulls' spread plus kyy.

Each field takes a value, a list (150, 200) or a range (150:250:25); every combination is a case. Its float at rest, GM and GZ curve are computed as it is made, a few seconds each.
