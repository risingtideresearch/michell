IGES and STL: the waterline is the design waterline height in the file's frame, in metres. STL carries no units, so give its scale.

Cases

Each field takes a value, a list (0.3, 0.4) or a range (0.3:0.6:0.05); every combination becomes a case. A span doubles the hull into a catamaran with its demihulls' centreplanes that far apart. Cases run one at a time; click a finished one to view it.

Thin-ship linear flow: the pressure on each hull (Cp = p / ½ρU²) and the free surface around the fleet, local field and waves together. Each case first floats the hull at its dynamic equilibrium (sinkage and trim at speed) — the load defaults to its design displacement at its LCB — and re-cuts it there. A transom is closed by the ballistic hollow, L_v = √2·U·√(d_T/g). A monohull case takes 5–40 s.
